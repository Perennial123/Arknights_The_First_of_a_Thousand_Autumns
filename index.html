This is a fan website to Hypergryth's game Arknights, specifically under the theme of the game patch "First of a Thousand Autumns." This is a non-commmecialized website intending to be a show of love to Arknights as a fan of the game, hoping to help Arknights to gain more audience.

Some context for audience who are not familiar with Arknights or its lore in the first place. Arknights can be described as a kind of sci-fi tale set on a planet called Terra. There exists unique species that have different traits and special abilities. Sui is one of them.

Sui itself

Sui is powerful and is classified as a "Feranmut" in the world of Terra, a type of creature that has abilities beyond what one can imagine, and has almost endless lifespan.

To describe Sui's story it would require a lot of details, but to put it simply, in its long living life, Sui has been the dominating one over other Feranmuts,

Sui's ambition to become the strongest Feranmut is what drives its living. To be the king, Sui spends its time fighting ferociously with tactics. At some point it comes across to Yanese, people who inhabits at a place called the Great Yan, and for the sake of Sui's goal, it decides to be in partnership with the Great Yan. The bond lasted until Sui was betrayed by its working partner.
its own kind when they are in a war with humans and chooses to turn sides. But in the end Sui experiences similar betrayal from the humanity, and despite its undying body, it is broken into twelve pieces, all of whom has their own consciounesses, powers and wills.

The twelve broken pieces of Sui's are generally recognized as the Sui Proxies. They have magical abilities just like Sui does. Beginning the moment of their birth, the Sui Proxies thinks of only one question: "Who am I?"

As the game's protagonist, Doctor, we are the major leader of a pharmaceutical company located inside a moving landship called Rhodes Island. From this perspective, we don't actually interact with Sui, but a majority of Sui's Proxies are companions of Rhodes Island's after going through various interesting stories. The First of a Thousand Autumns are actually not within Doctor's scope of story, rather it stands as the ninth subsidestory of the Through the Ages sidestory in Arknights' lores. Without further saying, let's dive into the story of "First of a Thousand Autumns".

The story of Sui Proxies : a Brief Introduction to the sidestory "Through the Ages"

Sui Proxies' lives all started at the same time as Sui's weakening and beginning of its long sleep.

Twelve new souls were born and each with magnificent ability inherited from the Sui itself, but eager to find an answer to the question: "Who am I?"

Without almost endless lives, the twelve Sui Proxies spends their time on figuring out their identities, and things that they wish to do. The Proxies sees each other as siblings, with their rankings ordered by the amount of time they used to finding their identities.
